AI Connection Discuss Bot - Accounting

Finance tools for the AI bot of ai_connection_discuss_bot. The bot answers questions about the company’s cash flow with figures computed by Odoo; the AI only explains them.

Tool Answers
cash_flow_forecast Opening cash, money in and out per week or month by category, overdue items, running balance and the lowest point. Optionally “realistic”: open customer invoices moved by each customer’s average payment delay.
cash_flow_history Actual cash flow statement per month from the bank and cash accounts, by category: customers, suppliers, taxes, wages and other liabilities, other operating, investments, financing. Internal transfers are left out.

The forecast uses open invoices and bills and the forecast lines of mis_builder_cash_flow (mis_builder_cash_flow_auto and its sales, purchase and contract modules generate them).

Like all tools of the bot, they run with the access rights of the user who asked: a user who cannot read journal items gets no figures.

Configuration

Installing the module adds the two tools to the default bot (Claude). For another bot, add cash_flow_forecast and cash_flow_history to its AI Bot Tools (Settings > Users > the bot > AI Bot).

Set up the forecast sources as described in mis_builder_cash_flow_auto (recurring costs, VAT). Bank and cash accounts with Hide in Cash Flow are left out of both tools.

Usage

Ask the bot in Discuss, for example:

  • What does our cash look like for the next 13 weeks?
  • When is our cash at its lowest in the coming 12 months, and why?
  • Make the forecast realistic: how late do our customers pay?
  • Show the cash flow statement of the past 6 months. Where did the money go?

Known issues / Roadmap

  • Financing and investments are recognised from account types only; short-term loans on current liability accounts are counted with wages and other liabilities.
  • The realistic forecast moves customer invoices only, not the forecast lines of sales orders and contracts.
